Cozy Pond Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to cozybossgames@gmail.com — we usually answer within 2 days.

How do I play?
Drag your finger through the dirt to dig tunnels. Water flows downhill: lead it to Vırak's dry pond. Rocks can't be dug, and green moss soaks up water, so go around them.
How do I get 3 fireflies?
Fireflies light up when water touches them. Shape your tunnel so the water passes through all three on its way to the pond.
I'm stuck on a level.
Tap the round restart button under the level number to try again with fresh dirt.
Turn the music off
Tap the music note button in the top corner of the map.
