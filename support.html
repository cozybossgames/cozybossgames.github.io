Cozy Rocket Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to cozybossgames@gmail.com — we usually answer within 2 days.

I bought the No Ads Pack but still see ads.
Open the game, tap NO ADS on the main menu and choose RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you use the same Apple ID you bought it with.
How do I land on the Moon?
Tap the fire button in short bursts near the ground and keep the rocket upright. Captain Cotton makes landing easier.
Change language
Main menu → SETTINGS → pick English, Türkçe, Español or Português.
