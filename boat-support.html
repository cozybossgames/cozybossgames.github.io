Cozy Boat Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to cozybossgames@gmail.com — we usually answer within 2 days.

I bought the No Ads Pack but still see ads.
Open the game, tap the yellow NO ADS button in the shipyard and choose RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you use the same Apple ID you bought it with.
How do I stop capsizing?
Lift your finger while the boat is in the air: it levels itself before landing. Captain Pakpak makes capsizing harder.
Change language
Tap the language button (TR / EN / ES / PT) at the top of the shipyard.
