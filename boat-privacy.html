Privacy Policy — Cozy Boat

Last updated: October 30, 2026. Cozy Boat is made by Cozy Boss.

Cozy Boat does not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising. The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to show and measure ads. On iPhone and iPad you are asked for permission before your advertising ID is used for tracking; if you decline, you will see non-personalized ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the game's shipyard. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
